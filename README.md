#  Why I Love Marvel

I love Marvel because I’ve always been fascinated by supernatural stories. The Marvel series is huge and exciting to watch. What I love most is that—even with all its supernatural elements—Marvel Studios helps me believe that the inner child in me never dies. Growing older doesn’t mean you have to let go of your imagination or sense of wonder.

##My Favorite Character: Spider-Man

My favorite Marvel character is **Spider-Man**. While I admire Tom Holland’s version, my favorite portrayal is by **Andrew Garfield**. I especially love Spider-Man because of the sacrifices he makes to protect his family and loved ones. I relate to that deeply—I'm willing to make sacrifices for the people I care about too.

I also connect with **Peter Parker** as a person. The way he carries his problems and tries to fix them, even when it’s hard, reminds me of myself. He’s not just a superhero—he’s someone who struggles, feels pain, and keeps going. That’s what makes him truly inspiring.

> “With great power comes great responsibility.” – Uncle Ben

##  Why Marvel Matters to Me
- It keeps my imagination alive.
- It reminds me that being an adult doesn’t mean losing your inner child.
- It inspires me to be brave, selfless, and resilient.
